# Van Life — 2004 Ford E350 Conversion Design

This repo uses image-blaster skills to design and visualize the van conversion.

## The Van

- **Base:** 2004 Ford E350 Extended Cargo Van
- **Goal:** Full-time van life conversion — sleep, work, cook, live

## Design Areas

- Interior layout (bed platform, desk, storage, kitchen, electrical)
- Exterior wrap / paint concept
- Materials and fixtures selection

## How to Use

Put reference images in `input/` and ask Claude to analyze and blast them.

Use `image-blast-uncover` to analyze any van interior reference image.
Use `image-blast-image-edit` to modify and iterate on design concepts.
Use `image-blast-world` to generate 3D explorable environments of interior layouts.
Use `image-blast-3d` to generate 3D models of specific fixtures (bed frame, desk, cabinets).

## Van Dimensions (E350 Extended Cargo)

- Interior length: ~12.5 ft (150 inches)
- Interior width: ~70 inches (58.5 inches between wheel wells)
- Interior height: ~72 inches (high roof) / ~51 inches (standard)
- Payload: 2,960 lbs
