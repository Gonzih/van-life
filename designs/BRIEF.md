# E350 Conversion Brief

## Constraints

- Extended cargo van — 150" interior length, 70" wide, wheel wells at 58.5"
- Standard roof (51" interior height) — no high-top on this build
- Full-time live-in: must sleep 2 adults comfortably
- Must be able to work remotely: standing desk or sit-stand

## Priority Order

1. Bed platform (fixed, queen-width across the van)
2. Electrical system (lithium, solar, shore power)  
3. Kitchen (2-burner propane, sink, 12V fridge)
4. Desk / work area
5. Storage (under bed, overhead, garage in back)

## Aesthetic Direction

- Clean, minimal, Scandinavian-ish
- Light wood tones + matte black hardware
- Not overlander / tactical — livable and calm

## Reference Vibes

- Sprinter build quality, E350 budget
- Studio apartment, not a camper
